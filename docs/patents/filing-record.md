## 🔴 Disposition: D is planned to lapse

**Only B is being converted to a nonprovisional.** The goal is a granted patent
as a durable credential rather than revenue, and one conversion delivers that
as well as four.

D ranked last of the four for conversion. Several of its aspects reduce to
"refuse the write unless this field is present," which examiners readily
characterize as abstract data organization under §101, and it overlaps quipu's
governed-store substrate enough to raise double-patenting questions if both
were ever pursued.

**If nothing changes, D lapses on 2027-08-17.**
